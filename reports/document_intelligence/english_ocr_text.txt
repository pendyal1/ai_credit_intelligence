Chairman's Letter

Dear valued shareholders,

It is my pleasure to present the annual report of El Nile Textile and Export Company for the

fiscal year ended 31 December 2024. This year brought significant challenges and opportunities,
and our company succeeded in achieving notable revenue growth of 18 percent compared to the
previous year.

The company's revenue this year reached 487,350 thousand Egyptian pounds, compared to 412,
thousand in the prior year. The company achieved net income of 64,530 thousand pounds, an
increase of 20 percent over the previous year.

Our exports to European and Gulf markets represent 72 percent of total sales. We are pleased

to announce our entry into new markets in Germany and the United Kingdom during this year.
On the balance sheet side, total assets reached 721,350 thousand pounds, with notable
improvement in working capital and a reduction in long-term debt.

We thank our valued shareholders for their continued support and look forward to 2025 with
confidence and optimism.

With sincere regards,

Mohammed Abdel Rahman EI Sherif

Chairman of the Board

Management Discussion and Analysis
Performance Overview
Fiscal year 2024 saw strong growth in business volume, with sales rising 18 percent compared
to the prior year. This growth is attributed to our expansion into new export markets and
improved raw material pricing.
Gross profit margin reached 35.8 percent, compared to 34.9 percent in the prior year,
reflecting improvements in production efficiency and cost discipline.
The main challenges the company faced during the year included foreign exchange rate
volatility, where we recorded FX losses of 8,310 thousand pounds.

Management Discussion and Analysis (continued)
Future Outlook

Management expects growth to continue in fiscal year 2025, supported by rising demand for
Egyptian textile products in European markets.

The company plans to invest 85,000 thousand pounds in expanding production capacity during tt
first quarter of next year.

The company is also working to diversify its customer base and reduce sales concentration on a
single customer; the largest customer currently represents 32 percent of total sales.

Key risks include cotton price volatility in global markets, competition from Asian
manufacturers, and the impact of international trade policies.

Notes to Financial Statements

Note 1: About the Company

El Nile Textile and Export Company is an Egyptian joint stock company established on 3 March
1998 under the provisions of Law No. 159 of 1981. The company's head office is located in the
Tenth of Ramadan Industrial Zone, Sharqia Governorate, Arab Republic of Egypt. The principal
activity of the company is the manufacture and export of cotton textiles and ready-made
garments.

Note 2: Significant Accounting Policies

a. Basis of preparation: The financial statements have been prepared in accordance with

Egyptian Accounting Standards.

b. Functional currency: The Egyptian pound is the functional and presentation currency of the
company.

c. Fixed assets: Fixed assets are stated at cost less accumulated depreciation. Depreciation

is calculated using the straight-line method over the estimated useful life of the asset

(buildings 40 years, machinery and equipment 10 years, furniture and fixtures 5 years).

d. Inventory: Inventory is valued at the lower of cost or net realisable value. Cost is

determined using the weighted average method.

e. Trade receivables: Trade receivables are stated at nominal value less provision for

doubtful debts.

Independent Auditor's Report
To the Shareholders of El Nile Textile and Export Company,
We have audited the accompanying financial statements of El Nile Textile and Export Company
(S.A.E.), which comprise the balance sheet as at 31 December 2024, and the statements of
income, cash flows and changes in equity for the year then ended, along with a summary of
significant accounting policies and the accompanying notes.
Our audit was conducted in accordance with Egyptian Auditing Standards. We performed our audit
to obtain reasonable assurance that the financial statements are free of material misstatement.
Opinion: In our opinion, based on the audit we conducted, the accompanying financial
statements present fairly, in all material respects, the financial position of the company as
at 31 December 2024, and the results of its operations and its cash flows for the year then
ended, in accordance with Egyptian Accounting Standards.
Mahmoud Hassan El Feqi
Certified Public Accountant, Fellow of the Egyptian Society of Accountants and Auditors
